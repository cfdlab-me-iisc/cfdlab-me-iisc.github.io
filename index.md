The Computational Dynamics Lab studies systems in which simple local rules
produce complex behaviour: chemicals that organise into spots and stripes,
waves scattering through structured materials, and fluids that mix
chaotically even when they move slowly.

Our work combines mathematical analysis with large-scale simulation. We
write and maintain open-source solvers, and we collaborate with
experimental groups to test our predictions in the laboratory.

We are part of the Department of Applied Mathematics at Example University.
If you are interested in joining us, see the [Contact page](/contact/).
